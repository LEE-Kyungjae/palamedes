# Rareness, recombination, and technology value

This folder is a research packet for evaluating Palamedes' discovery and
candidate-selection design against work on technological rareness, recombinant
search, local search, knowledge maturity, and novelty measurement.

## What is actually stored locally

| Paper | Local artifact | Access/read status |
|---|---|---|
| Fleming (2001), *Recombinant Uncertainty in Technological Search* | `papers/fleming-2001-recombinant-uncertainty.pdf` | Author-hosted published PDF; full paper read |
| Stuart & Podolny (1996), *Local Search and the Evolution of Technological Capabilities* | `papers/stuart-podolny-1996-local-search.pdf` | Author-hosted published PDF; full paper read |
| Uzzi et al. (2013), *Atypical Combinations and Scientific Impact* | `papers/uzzi-et-al-2013-atypical-combinations.pdf` | Author-hosted manuscript with supplementary material; full paper and methods read |

The focal paper, Lee, Jung, and Park (2023), is paywalled. Its publisher
abstract, bibliographic record, hypotheses, and reported findings were checked,
but a local full-text PDF was not obtained. The same distinction is preserved
for Capaldo et al. (2017). Fontana et al. (2020) was read from its accessible
repository/web text, but the repository rejected automated local download.

Do not describe the papers in the second group as locally archived or fully
verified from a downloaded publisher PDF.

## Files

- `palamedes-insights.md`: synthesis, current-system comparison, gaps, and
  proposed evaluation design.
- `sources.bib`: bibliography and stable source links.
- `papers/`: legally accessible author-hosted PDFs only.

## Scope warning

Most evidence in this packet concerns patents or scholarly publications. It
supports design hypotheses for Palamedes, not a claim that citation impact,
patent rareness, or a fitted inverted-U optimum transfers directly to mission
discovery by an AI system.
